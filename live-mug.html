Questions

Good to know

Do they need to download an app?

No. They scan the code on the mug with the phone camera, tap once, allow the camera and point it at the photo.

Which phones does it work on?

Any recent iPhone or Android phone with a camera and a browser.

How long can the video be?

Up to 60 seconds. We compress it so it loads quickly on any connection.

When will it arrive?

Mugs are printed in the USA and usually arrive 3–5 business days after you order.

Can I change the video later?

Yes, with Full editing. Upload a new video any time and the same mug plays it — no reprint needed. The printed photo stays the same.

Can you send it straight to someone as a gift?

Yes. Turn on “Ship it straight to them”, add their name and address, and we send the mug in a gift box with no prices inside. You can add a short gift note.

What can’t I upload?

No sexual content, bullying, hate, violence, illegal activity, other people’s private information or content you don’t have rights to. Every video is checked before printing.

Is my video private?

Each mug gets its own private link that nobody can guess. Only people with the mug can open it.
